Standard Any% Glitchless Splits
Speedrunners generally split their timer at these major milestones in the run:
Brock: First major gym leader milestone.
Mt. Moon: Clearing the iconic cave area.
Rival (or Nugget Bridge): Early game resource and rival checks.
Misty: Second gym leader.
Lt. Surge: Third gym leader.
Erika: Fourth gym leader.
Koga: Fifth gym leader.
Sabrina: Sixth gym leader.
Blaine: Seventh gym leader.
Giovanni: Eighth gym leader and final hideout check.
Hall of Fame: Stopping the timer on the final screen of the game.
