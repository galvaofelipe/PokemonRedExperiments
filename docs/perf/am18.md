# AM18 (win-p2kh1a2oie9) — performance manifest

Measured 2026-09-13. Convention: `docs/perf/README.md`. Compare SPS only across
machines — mem/cpu columns here are Linux-specific (PSS footprint, interval cpu_pct).

## Hardware / config

- Ryzen 7 PRO 8845HS (8C/16T), 30 GB RAM, Radeon 780M iGPU (no CUDA).
- WSL2 Ubuntu 26.04: `.wslconfig` = 24 GB RAM, 16 procs, 16 GB swap, `vmIdleTimeout=-1`.
- Python 3.12.14 (uv venv), torch 2.5.0+**cpu**, SB3 2.3.2.
- Telemetry: `v2/resource_callback.py` (fixed 2026-09-13) — footprint = PSS sum over
  the process tree (smaps_rollup), cpu_pct = interval CPU from /proc stat deltas
  (100% = one core). The RSS column double-counts fork-shared pages; ignore it for
  sizing.

## Raw physical sweep (stock geometry, n_steps 2560; 2–5 min bursts)

Harness: `v2/bench_sps.sh` → `v2/bench/bench_results.csv`.

| envs | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| total SPS | 188 | 303 | 495 | 744 | 749 | 825 | 909 | 1033 | 1047 | **1094** | 1025 |

- Hard plateau at 9–10 envs (= physical cores); SPS/env decays past that.
- 32-env dip coincided with memory pressure (sum > 24 GB cap).
- Peak total throughput: 28 envs. Best throughput/RAM: ~20 envs.

## Geometry bench (2 mega-updates per cell; `v2/bench/bench_geometry.csv`)

| cell | logical/physical | avg SPS | peak footprint (PSS) | peak CPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| g2560 (t05 A) | 8/8, n=2560 | 740 | 5.9 GB | 769% |
| g20480 (t05 B) | 8/8, n=20480 | 526 | 22.9 GB | 777% |
| acc40_p8 | 40/8, n=2560 | 738 | 19.0 GB | 772% |
| acc40_p20 | 40/20, n=2560 | **1008** | 21.4 GB | 821% |
| acc48_p16 | 48/16, n=2560 | 896 | 22.8 GB | 780% |
| acc64_p8 (t16) | 64/8, n=2560 | 715 | 22.9 GB | 770% |
| acc64_p16 | 64/16, n=2560 | 863 | 22.9 GB | 781% |
| acc80_p16 | 80/16, n=2560 | **DNF** | — | swap death, see below |

**acc80 does not fit the 24 GB cap:** the 204,800-sample buffer (~21 GB) + 16 envs
exhausted RAM **and** the 16 GB swap at 98.5% of the 2nd mega-update — the whole WSL
VM hung (sps=0, CPU sagging to ~50%, 7+ min of thrash until `wsl --shutdown`).
acc80_p20 was not attempted (same buffer + 4 envs). Failure mode is a slow swap
spiral, not a quick OOM-kill — do not retry without the ticket-18 diet.

Calibrated rule of thumb: **buffer ≈ 105 KB/sample** (logical × n_steps) **+ ~0.6 GB
per physical env**. At the 24 GB cap: 64 logical is the practical ceiling (on the
edge); 80 logical is impossible — even a 27 GB `.wslconfig` doesn't close it
(21.5 GB buffer + envs).

## Readings

- A 163,840-sample mega-update buffer costs **~17 GB** on this box. 64-stream
  geometries peak ~22.9 GB PSS — ~1 GB from the cap. Not week-long safe unmitigated.
- B (g20480) is strictly dominated: slowest AND at the RAM ceiling.
- acc40_p20 is the throughput/safety sweet spot (1008 SPS, ~2.5 GB below the cap).
- g2560 (A) and acc40_p8 are the only cells with wide RAM headroom.
- Peak CPU ~7.7–8.2 of 16 threads in every cell — serial-head ceiling, not geometry.

## Recommended production geometries

- **Throughput pick: acc40_p20** — 1008 SPS with margin; 40-stream mega-update
  (102,400 samples) keeps the buffer at ~10.5 GB.
- **If 64 streams is required (ticket-15 default): acc64_p16**, but ONLY with one of:
  `.wslconfig` raised to 26–27 GB; accumulator diet (ticket 18: memmap obs — `del`
  after concat does NOT help, the chained buffer reads obs from live round buffers
  during train); or drop to 40–48 logical streams.
- **80 streams:** only after the memmap diet lands.
- **Raw safe throughput:** stock geometry at 16–20 envs.

## Caveats

- All numbers are 3–10 min bursts. Expect ~10–20% SPS sag over week-long thermal soak.
- **Pending:** 30–60 min steady-state validation at the chosen geometry before any
  marathon run.
