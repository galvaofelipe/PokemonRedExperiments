# AM18 (win-p2kh1a2oie9) — performance manifest

Measured 2026-09-13. Convention: `docs/perf/README.md`. Compare SPS only across
machines — mem/cpu columns here are Linux-specific (PSS footprint, interval cpu_pct).

## Hardware / config

- Ryzen 7 PRO 8845HS (8C/16T), 32 GB RAM (30.8 GB usable Windows-side after the
  2026-09-13 BIOS UMA-framebuffer shrink; 4 GB was hardware-reserved), Radeon
  780M iGPU (no CUDA).
- WSL2 Ubuntu 26.04: `.wslconfig` = 27 GB RAM, 16 procs, 32 GB swap,
  `vmIdleTimeout=-1` (raised 2026-09-13 evening from 24 GB / 16 GB; geometry
  table below was measured under the OLD cap except where noted).
- Python 3.12.14 (uv venv), torch 2.5.0+**cpu**, SB3 2.3.2.
- Telemetry: `v2/resource_callback.py` (fixed 2026-09-13) — footprint = PSS sum over
  the process tree (smaps_rollup), cpu_pct = interval CPU from /proc stat deltas
  (100% = one core). The RSS column double-counts fork-shared pages; ignore it for
  sizing.

**acc80 does not fit the 24 GB cap:** the 204,800-sample buffer (~21 GB) + 16 envs
exhausted RAM **and** the 16 GB swap at 98.5% of the 2nd mega-update — the whole WSL
VM hung (sps=0, CPU sagging to ~50%, 7+ min of thrash until `wsl --shutdown`).
acc80_p20 was not attempted (same buffer + 4 envs). Failure mode is a slow swap
spiral, not a quick OOM-kill — do not retry without the ticket-18 diet.

Calibrated rule of thumb: **buffer ≈ 105 KB/sample** (logical × n_steps) **+ ~0.6 GB
per physical env**. At the 24 GB cap: 64 logical is the practical ceiling (on the
edge); 80 logical is impossible — even a 27 GB `.wslconfig` doesn't close it
(21.5 GB buffer + envs).

## External validation (system view, outside the process)

Same acc64_p8 geometry re-run with an independent sampler (`scripts/mem_watch.py`):
system-wide `/proc/meminfo` + externally-walked tree PSS every 15 s.

- **Telemetry cross-check passes:** external tree PSS peak 22,785 MB vs in-process
  22,818 MB (0.2% apart).
- **PSS hides swap:** at the train-phase peak, `MemAvailable` bottomed at **218 MB**
  and swap usage jumped 0.4 → **5.3 GB**. True tree demand ≈ 27–28 GB — above the
  24 GB RAM. acc64 at this cap does not fit; it survives only because swap absorbs
  the overflow, at a visible cost (instant SPS ~870 → ~690–760, ~15%).
- Swap leaves a multi-GB residue after the peak — expect this to accumulate over a
  long run.
- Consequence: for 64-stream geometries a mitigation (`.wslconfig` 26–27 GB or the
  ticket-18 memmap diet) is **required, not optional**. acc40_p20 (demand ~22.8 GB)
  is the largest geometry that stays honest at the 24 GB cap.

## Accumulator RAM diet (ticket 23, measured 2026-09-14)

Implementation: `v2/native_dtype_rollout_buffer.py` + free-after-train in
`v2/accumulator_ppo.py`. Smoke: `v2/mem_audit_t21.py --tag post_diet`
(8 logical / 4 physical, n_steps 256, 4 mega-updates). Per-sample nbytes is
exact (array introspection); acc64 GB figures scale the same per-sample
numbers as ticket 21.

| | before (ticket 21) | after (post_diet) |
|---|---|---|
| obs storage | float32 every key | space.dtype (screens/map uint8, events/badges int8, rest float32/int64) |
| obs nbytes / 1024-sample round | **90,488,832** | **22,671,360** |
| B/sample (obs only) | 88,368 | 22,140 |
| B/sample (chain total, + arrays + concat) | 88,408 | 22,180 |
| acc64 buffer (163,840 samples) | 14.48 GB | 3.63 GB |
| collection peak | 2× chain (double-alive until next reassignment) | 1× chain (previous dropped after `train()`) |

PSS timeline on the smoke: after MU1 train, collection of MU2/3/4 stays flat at
the post-train PSS (525 MB) — no second chain during collection. Ticket 21's
verify run on the same geometry climbed 495→1200 MB across MUs with the
measurement wrapper; production `--no-batch-wrap` was already flat, but at
float32 size. Losses/SPS logged normally on the smoke (entropy ≈ −1.95).

Projected acc64_p16 with both levers (ticket 21 scaling, not a full acc64
re-bench): ~9 GB train peak / ~12.5 GB collection — under the 27 GB WSL cap
without swap. 80 logical streams is now a geometry question (ticket 22), not a
memmap prerequisite.

## Readings

- A 163,840-sample mega-update buffer costs **~17 GB** on this box **before the
  ticket-23 diet**; **3.63 GB** after (22,180 B/sample). 64-stream geometries
  peaked ~22.9 GB PSS unmitigated — that figure is historical.
- B (g20480) is strictly dominated: slowest AND at the RAM ceiling.
- acc40_p20 is the throughput/safety sweet spot (1008 SPS, ~2.5 GB below the cap).
- g2560 (A) and acc40_p8 are the only cells with wide RAM headroom.
- Peak CPU ~7.7–8.2 of 16 threads in every cell — serial-head ceiling, not geometry.

## Recommended production geometries

- **Throughput pick: acc40_p20** — 1008 SPS with margin; 40-stream mega-update
  (102,400 samples) is ~2.3 GB of buffer after the diet (was ~10.5 GB float32).
- **If 64 streams is required (ticket-15 default): acc64_p16.** Ticket 23 diet
  (uint8 obs + free-after-train) brings the buffer from 14.48 → 3.63 GB;
  projected peak ~9 GB train / ~12.5 GB collect, under the 27 GB cap. Confirm
  with a short acc64 re-bench before a marathon — the 22.9 GB / swap figures
  above are pre-diet.
- **80 streams:** no longer blocked on memmap; still wants a geometry bench
  (ticket 22).
- **Raw safe throughput:** stock geometry at 16–20 envs.

## Caveats

- All numbers are 3–10 min bursts. Expect ~10–20% SPS sag over week-long thermal soak.
- **Pending:** 30–60 min steady-state validation at the chosen geometry before any
  marathon run.
